Paper Map

A browser-local bibliography, directed citation graph and thematic research atlas. Your working library stays in IndexedDB on this browser unless you explicitly export it.

PDF citations can be extracted locally with Rust/WebAssembly, DOI/arXiv identifiers can be resolved through Crossref and Semantic Scholar, and papers can carry reviewed semantic research relationships such as “improves on”, “invalidates”, or “outperforms”.
